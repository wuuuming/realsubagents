**v0.0 baseline** (`reports/bench/001-baseline.md`): 32/32 accepted on 13 tasks, $50.19.
- Opus 5.5 prices: cacheRead $0.2, cacheWrite $5, output $20 per Mtok.
- Cost model `src/costmodel.ts`, fit with `bench/fit.ts`. Per-task held-out error median 24%; per-class aggregate within ±3% (H-1 redefined per class, D-25); run-to-run noise (CV) 12%.

**v0.05 dry run, prompts v0** (`reports/bench/002-dryrun.md`): 4 large-parallel tasks × 3, 12/12 accepted. Median cost 99% and time 86% of baseline, so G0.05 NO-GO as written; orchestrator share 10%, serial fraction 41%.
- **Time causes:** each worker re-runs slow suites (repo-wide `npm run check`, full package suites), and the splits are unequal (balanced, the time ratio would be 71%). Editing itself parallelises well: 143 s per worker vs 373 s for the baseline.
- **Cost:** reads −$0.23 and realctx −$0.13 per run, cancelled by duplicated prefix writes (+$0.20) and extra output (+$0.13). A 20% cost saving needs ~150 calls per session (ours ~59).
